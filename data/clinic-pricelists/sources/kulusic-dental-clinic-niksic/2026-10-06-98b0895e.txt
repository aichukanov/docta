Dental Studio dr Kulusic
Dr Kulusic Dental Studio is the place to go for dental care of various specialties, from the simplest preventive treatments to the most complex functional – aesthetic rehabilitations.
Based on a dental examination and review of x-ray scan, we will assess your dental condition and propose a plan and course of further treatment. You will be provided with a written Cost Estimate, including different therapy options, précised therapy phases, and the exact number of visits, duration of treatments, and prices.
