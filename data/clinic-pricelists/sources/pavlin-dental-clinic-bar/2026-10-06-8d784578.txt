What are dental veneers?
Dental veneers are thin coverings applied to the front surface of teeth to improve their appearance. They can change the colour, shape, size or length of a tooth - and when done well, the results are completely natural-looking. Veneers are one of the most powerful tools in cosmetic dentistry for transforming a smile.
At Pavlin dental clinic in Bar, we specialise in direct composite veneers - a technique where tooth-coloured composite resin is sculpted directly onto the tooth surface by your dentist in a single appointment. No laboratory needed, no waiting weeks - your new smile is ready the same day.

What can veneers fix?
✓ Chipped or cracked teeth
Restore the natural shape and protect the tooth surface.
✓ Persistent discolouration
Stains that do not respond to whitening - including internal discolouration from tetracycline or trauma.
✓ Gaps between teeth
Close small diastemas (gaps) without orthodontic treatment.
✓ Irregular shape or size
Reshape teeth that are too small, uneven or have an unusual form.
✓ Worn-down teeth
Restore length and thickness to teeth worn by grinding or acid erosion.

🏺 Porcelain veneers (indirect)
We refer to trusted specialists
◦ Made in a dental laboratory - takes 2+ appointments
◦ Very thin, highly aesthetic ceramic shells
◦ Requires permanent removal of a thin layer of enamel
◦ Not reversible
◦ Can last 10-15 years
◦ Higher cost - from 300€+
If porcelain veneers are the right choice for you, we will refer you to a trusted prosthetics specialist.

Frequently asked questions
How long do composite veneers last?
+
With proper care, direct composite veneers typically last 5 to 8 years. They can be polished, repaired or replaced as needed - unlike porcelain veneers, which are permanent and cannot be repaired if chipped. Avoiding biting very hard foods (ice, nuts, hard crusts) and maintaining good oral hygiene are the most important factors in longevity.
Do veneers damage your teeth?
+
Direct composite veneers are a minimally invasive option - in many cases no tooth structure needs to be removed at all, or only a tiny amount of enamel is etched for better bonding. This is one of the major advantages of the composite technique over traditional porcelain veneers, which always require permanent enamel removal. Your dentist will explain exactly what preparation is needed for your specific case at the examination.
Can I eat normally with composite veneers?
+
Yes, absolutely. You can eat normally after a short initial period (we advise avoiding very hard or sticky foods for the first 24 hours while the composite fully sets). After that, normal eating is fine - just be mindful of very hard foods like ice or hard nuts, which can chip any dental restoration, natural or not.
Can I get veneers on just one or two teeth?
+
Yes - you do not need a full smile makeover. Many of our patients come to us for a single chipped tooth or a couple of discoloured teeth. Your dentist will carefully shade-match the composite to your natural teeth so the veneer blends in completely. We will be honest with you about what is and is not achievable on one tooth versus multiple teeth.
