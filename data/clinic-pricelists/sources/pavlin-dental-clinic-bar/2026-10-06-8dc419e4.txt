Do you offer dental implants?
+
Dental implants require specialised oral surgery and a dedicated implant center. We do not perform implant surgery at our clinic. However, if you need dental implants, we assess your situation, help you understand all your options, and refer you to trusted implant and oral surgery specialists in Montenegro whose work we have seen and recommend. We only refer to professionals we trust personally with our own patients.
Do you perform oral surgery?
+
Routine tooth extractions are part of our services. For complex oral surgery - such as wisdom teeth removal under general anaesthesia, cyst removal, or bone grafting - we work closely with qualified oral surgery specialists and will provide a thorough referral with full information about your case. Your oral health is in good hands from start to finish.
Do you make permanent dental crowns or prosthetics?
+
We offer temporary crowns and composite restorations in our dental clinic. For permanent ceramic crowns, bridges and full prosthetics, we collaborate with trusted prosthetics specialists and laboratories. We prepare your tooth and coordinate everything - so you get the best possible results from a team that works together.

How often should I see a dentist?
+
For most people, visiting the dentist every 6 months for a check-up and professional teeth cleaning is the best way to maintain oral health. Children should see a dentist at least once a year. If you have gum disease or other conditions, your dentist may recommend more frequent visits. Early care prevents bigger problems - and bigger costs - later. Most health guidelines from leading medical organisations worldwide recommend this schedule.
What should I do in a dental emergency?
+
Call our dental clinic immediately at 068-859-801 . We are available 24/7 for dental emergencies - evenings, weekends and public holidays included. Acute tooth pain, a broken tooth, lost filling, dental abscess: don't suffer, call us right away. Prompt dental treatment prevents more serious oral health problems down the line.
