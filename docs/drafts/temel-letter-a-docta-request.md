## Verzija na crnogorskom / srpskom (latinica)

**Predmet:** Zahtjev redakcije docta.me za komentar prije objavljivanja materijala

Poštovani,

obraćamo Vam se iz redakcije docta.me, onlajn kataloga medicinskih ustanova u Crnoj Gori (https://docta.me).

Pripremamo materijal o dokumentovanom slučaju koji se odnosi na Vašu kliniku (Temel Dental, Donja Gorica, Bulevar 21. maj, Podgorica). Prema prepisci i platnim dokumentima u koje je redakcija imala uvid: roditelju maloljetnog pacijenta usmeno je saopšten plan ortodontskog liječenja ukupne vrijednosti oko 2.000 €, nakon čega je uplaćen avans od 1.000 €; nakon uplate klinika je pisanim putem dostavila plan liječenja ukupne vrijednosti 4.000 €, sa drugačijim sastavom liječenja; zahtjev za povraćaj sredstava za usluge koje nisu pružene je odbijen.

Prije objavljivanja materijala želimo da Vam omogućimo da iznesete zvaničan stav klinike. Molimo Vas i za odgovore na sljedeća pitanja:

1. Zašto ukupna cijena i sastav liječenja nisu bili pisano utvrđeni prije uplate?
2. Zašto je ukupan iznos od 4.000 € prvi put saopšten tek nakon uplate 1.000 €?
3. Zašto se u klinici ne praktikuju zaključivanje ugovora i izdavanje pisanog plana liječenja?
4. Zašto nije izdat fiskalni račun za 200 € plaćenih gotovinom?
5. Po kom osnovu je odbijen povraćaj sredstava za usluge koje nisu pružene?
6. Kakva je politika klinike u pogledu informisanja pacijenata o cijenama prije početka liječenja?

Odgovor očekujemo u roku od 7 kalendarskih dana od datuma ovog dopisa. Vaš odgovor biće objavljen u materijalu u cjelini ili u sažetom obliku. Ukoliko odgovor ne dobijemo u navedenom roku, materijal će biti objavljen uz napomenu da klinika nije odgovorila na zahtjev za komentar.

Takođe Vas obavještavamo da će na stranici Vaše klinike na docta.me biti objavljen odjeljak „Reputacija“. Klinika u svakom trenutku može dostaviti zvaničan odgovor za taj odjeljak — on će biti objavljen.

## English version (for the head office)

**Subject:** Request for comment from the docta.me editorial team ahead of publication

Dear Sir or Madam,

We are writing from the editorial team of docta.me, an online directory of medical facilities in Montenegro (https://docta.me).

We are preparing an article about a documented case involving your clinic (Temel Dental, Donja Gorica, Bulevar 21 Maj, Podgorica). According to correspondence and payment records reviewed by our editorial team: a parent of a minor patient was verbally quoted an orthodontic treatment plan totalling approximately €2,000, after which an advance payment of €1,000 was made; after the payment, the clinic sent a written treatment plan totalling €4,000 with a different scope of treatment; a request for a refund for services that were not provided was declined.

Before publication, we would like to give the clinic the opportunity to present its official position. We would also appreciate your answers to the following questions:

1. Why were the total price and the scope of treatment not put in writing before the payment was made?
2. Why was the total amount of €4,000 first communicated only after €1,000 had been paid?
3. Why does the clinic not conclude written contracts or issue written treatment plans?
4. Why was no fiscal receipt issued for the €200 paid in cash?
5. On what grounds was the refund declined for services that were not provided?
6. What is the clinic's policy on informing patients about prices before treatment begins?

We kindly ask for your reply within 7 calendar days of the date of this letter. Your response will be published in the article in full or in summary. If we receive no response within this period, the article will be published with a note that the clinic did not reply to our request for comment.

Please also note that the clinic's page on docta.me will include a "Reputation" section. The clinic may submit an official response for that section at any time — it will be published.
